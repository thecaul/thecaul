WHAT THE CAUL IS

A remembering space for men.

The Caul exists to create meaningful spaces for men to gather, learn, practice presence, and remember themselves in the company of other men.

This is not a lecture. It is not therapy. It is a room where you can arrive as you are, sit with other men, and put down some of what you have been carrying alone.

The Caul is one room for men who are safe and ready to do the work, whoever they are and whoever they love.

The Circle is the heart of it. Other gatherings grow around it, and more will come as the community grows.

The Founder

JOSHUA
CAMPBELL

For nineteen years I have worked behind the chair as a hairstylist. The men who found their way to me were a handful, loyal and consistent, and they stayed because something in that room felt safe. Not soft. Safe. There is a difference. Most men never walk through the door at all. Not because they don’t need the space, but because they have been taught that needing space makes them less of a man.

I know that teaching intimately. I lived inside it.

The masculine tried to make me disappear. It told me I wasn’t enough. Wasn’t man enough. Didn’t belong. It did what it does to so many of us: handed me a version of myself that was smaller than the truth and asked me to call it home.

I didn’t. I refused to be small for others’ comfort.

I found my way back to myself anyway. Not because someone held the door open for me, but because I refused to stop looking for it. I remembered who I am. I found my way back to my lineage, to something older and truer than anything the modern world had handed me about what a man is supposed to be.

That remembering is what The Caul is built on.

I am a shamanic practitioner based in Richmond, Virginia. My work first cracked open in Tulum, and my formal training took me to Portugal. I am not teaching from a mountaintop. I am walking the path myself.

This work is not about fixing men. It is about witnessing them. That is how we return the masculine. Not alone. Not through performance. In the presence of men willing to remember together.
